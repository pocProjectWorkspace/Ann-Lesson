# Privacy notice

**Draft for legal review. Placeholders in [brackets] must be completed before publication.** Last updated: [date].

This notice explains, in plain words, what the Lesson Plan platform does with personal information. It is written for parents first, because most of the people the Platform holds information about are young children.

## The short version

- Your child's school decides to use the Platform and creates your child's account. The school is in charge of your child's information. We run the Platform for the school.
- We hold only what is needed to run a reading book app: your child's name, class, a login ID, which pages they read and when, the drawings they make, stickers and star ratings their teacher gives, and the teacher's voice recordings they listen to.
- We never show advertising, never sell or share children's information with anyone for marketing, and never use it to train artificial intelligence models.
- You can ask the school for a copy of everything we hold about your child, and the school can delete it at any time with one action.

## Who is responsible

Your child's school is the **controller** of your child's personal information: it decides what is collected and why. [Operator legal name] ("we") is the **processor**: we store and process the information only on the school's instructions, under a written data processing agreement. Questions about your child's information go to the school office first. You can also reach us at [privacy email].

## What we collect

**About students**

- Name, class, login ID and a password chosen by the school (stored only as a one-way hash).
- Reading activity: which books were opened, the page reached, the time each page was on screen while the book was open, and the days on which reading happened.
- Work made in the app: colouring and drawings on book pages, and answers to activities a teacher sends to the class.
- Rewards and assessment entered by the teacher: stickers with an optional note, and a 1 to 5 rating per subject.
- Early-support flags the Platform shows the teacher, such as "has not read for a week", which are derived from the reading activity above.

**About teachers, school staff and parents**

- Name, login ID or email, role, class assignments, and the actions taken in the app (for example "gave a sticker"), kept in an activity log the school can see.
- If the school uses single sign-on with Microsoft or Google, the identifier the provider returns and the provider's name. We do not receive the person's provider password.

**Technical information**

- Server logs with IP address, browser type and time, kept for up to [30] days for security and troubleshooting.
- A session cookie that keeps you signed in. We set no advertising or analytics cookies.
- On the mobile app, an optional push notification token if you allow notifications.

**What we do not collect:** location, photographs or video of children, biometric data, contact details of children, or any data from other apps or websites.

## Why we use it

- To run the service the school asked for: show the right books to the right class, remember the page a child reached, let teachers see who has read what, and let parents follow their own child.
- To keep the service secure and working.
- To send the notifications the school has chosen, such as "a new book was published" or "your child got a sticker".

We do not profile children for any purpose other than the early-support flags shown to their own teacher, and those flags never leave the school.

## Who can see what

- A **student** sees only their own books, progress and stickers.
- A **parent** sees only the children the school has linked to them.
- A **teacher** sees the students in the classes they are assigned to.
- A **school administrator** sees the whole school.
- Schools are isolated from each other; no school can see another school's data.
- Our operations staff can access data only to run, secure and support the service, under confidentiality obligations, and such access is logged.

## Where it is kept and who helps us

Data is hosted with [hosting provider, region] (application and database) and [object storage provider, region] (books, recordings and backups). ## How long we keep it

- While a child has an account: for as long as the school keeps the account.
- When the school deletes a child: everything about that child (progress, drawings, stickers, ratings, flags) is deleted immediately from the live database, and from backups within [30] days.
- When a school's subscription ends: the school can export its data for [30] days; after that all of the school's data is deleted.
- Server logs: up to [30] days. Backups: 30 days.

## Your rights and your child's rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your child's information, and to object to or restrict its use. The school can do all of this directly in the Platform: a one-file export of everything about a child, correction of names and classes, and deletion. Ask the school office. If you believe the school or we have not handled your child's information properly, you can complain to [the supervisory authority for your country].

## Children

The Platform is designed for use by young children under the supervision of their school and parents. We do not collect information from children for our own purposes, we do not allow children to communicate with strangers through the Platform, and children cannot publish anything outside their own school. Where local law requires parental consent before a child's account is created, the school obtains it.

## Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes; all traffic is encrypted; every request is checked against the person's role and school; files are served only to people entitled to see them; administrator actions are logged; and the database is backed up nightly. A fuller description is in the [Security summary](/legal/security).
