# Data processing agreement

**Draft for legal review. Placeholders in [brackets] must be completed before signature.** Version [1.0], [date].

This agreement is between the school named in the School Agreement ("the School", the controller) and [Operator legal name] ("the Operator", the processor). It forms part of the School Agreement and governs the processing of personal data by the Operator on the School's behalf when providing the Lesson Plan platform ("the Platform").

## 1. Subject matter, duration and purpose

- **Subject matter:** hosting and operating the Platform for the School.
- **Duration:** the term of the School Agreement plus the deletion period in clause 9.
- **Purpose:** to provide digital reading books to the School's students, record their reading progress and work, support teachers' assessment and rewards, and inform parents, as described in the School Agreement and the Privacy notice.
- **Nature:** storage, retrieval, display and transmission of data within the Platform; automated derivation of reading statistics and early-support flags shown only to the School's own staff.

## 2. Categories of data subjects and data

- **Students** (young children): name, class, login identifier, password hash, reading activity (books opened, pages reached, time per page, reading days), colouring and drawings, activity answers, stickers and teacher notes, subject ratings, early-support flags.
- **Parents or guardians:** name, login identifier, optional email, link to their child, notification history.
- **Staff:** name, login identifier or email, role, class assignments, single sign-on identifier where used, activity log entries.
- **Technical:** IP addresses and browser details in server logs, session identifiers, optional push notification tokens.

No special categories of personal data are intended to be processed. The School must not upload such data.

## 3. Instructions

The Operator processes personal data only on the School's documented instructions, which are: the School Agreement, this agreement, and the configuration the School's administrators make in the Platform. The Operator informs the School if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes applicable data protection law.

## 4. Confidentiality

The Operator ensures that persons authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive access only to the extent needed to operate, secure and support the Platform. Such access is logged.

## 5. Security

The Operator implements and maintains the technical and organisational measures described in the Security summary (Annex 2), including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, role and tenant checks on every request, logging of administrative actions, nightly backups, and prompt application of security updates.

## 6. Sub-processors

The School authorises the sub-processors listed in Annex 1. The Operator informs the School at least 30 days before adding or replacing a sub-processor; the School may object on reasonable grounds within that period, in which case the parties seek a solution and, failing that, the School may terminate the affected service. The Operator remains responsible for its sub-processors.

## 7. Assistance to the School

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Operator assists the School with:

- requests from data subjects, by providing the built-in export (one file with everything held about a child), correction and deletion functions, and by responding within [5] working days to requests that cannot be handled with those functions;
- security of processing, personal data breach notification, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation, by providing the information in its possession.

## 8. Personal data breaches

The Operator notifies the School without undue delay, and in any case within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the School's data, with the information available at that time, and keeps the School informed as more becomes known.

## 9. Return and deletion

On termination of the School Agreement the School may export its data for [30] days. After that period the Operator deletes all of the School's personal data from the live systems and from backups within a further 30 days, unless law requires retention. Deletion of an individual student by the School takes effect immediately in the live system and within 30 days in backups.

## 10. Audit

The Operator makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this agreement, including this agreement's annexes, the Security summary and relevant policies, and allows for and contributes to audits by the School or an auditor mandated by the School, at most once a year unless required by a supervisory authority or following a breach, on at least 30 days' notice and subject to confidentiality.

## 11. International transfers

The Operator processes the School's data in the regions listed in Annex 1 and does not transfer it elsewhere without the School's prior written agreement and appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

## 12. Liability and order of precedence

Liability is governed by the School Agreement. In case of conflict between this agreement and the School Agreement regarding the processing of personal data, this agreement prevails.
